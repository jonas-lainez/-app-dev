# The Mistress (2012)

> *"Hindi lahat ng nagmamahal, masaya. Hindi lahat ng masaya, nagmamahal."* — **Sari Alfonso**[^1]

***The Mistress*** is a 2012 Filipino romantic melodrama directed by Olivia Lamasan. Celebrating the 10^th^ showbiz anniversary of iconic love team **John Lloyd Cruz** and **Bea Alonzo**, the film explores an *"imperfect love story"* about a young woman caught between loyalty and genuine love.

## Cast & Characters (Nested List)

1. **Rosario "Sari" Alfonso** *(Bea Alonzo)*
   * Occupation: Seamstress
   * Role: Kept mistress of Rico Torres
2. **Frederico "JD / Erick" Torres Jr.** *(John Lloyd Cruz)*
   * Occupation: Architect
   * Role: Persistent admirer who falls for Sari
3. **Rico Torres** *(Ronaldo Valdez)*
   * Occupation: Wealthy businessman
   * Role: Married patriarch and Sari's benefactor
4. **Regina Torres** *(Hilda Koronel)*
   * Role: Rico’s dignified legal wife

## Themes (Definition List)

Unconditional Love
: Loving someone without expecting anything in return, even when circumstances make it painful.

Loyalty vs. Freedom
: The internal struggle between repaying a debt of gratitude (`utang na loob`) and choosing personal happiness.
